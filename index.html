🇰🇪 Kenya CBE Learning Guide

EduShelf is designed to organize learning around Kenya's competency-based approach.

Important: The live system should use the current KICD curriculum designs and approved materials as its authoritative curriculum source. Teachers and administrators should verify generated content against the relevant official curriculum design.

🎯 Learning Outcomes

AI prompts learners toward what they should know, understand and demonstrate.

🧠 Core Competencies

Activities can support communication and collaboration, critical thinking and problem solving, creativity and imagination, citizenship, digital literacy, learning to learn and self-efficacy.

🔎 Inquiry-Based Learning

AI can generate key inquiry questions, examples, activities and reflection prompts.

📊 Assessment

Teachers can create formative questions, tasks, checklists and rubric-style practice aligned to the selected learning outcome.

🌱 Values & PCIs

Learning activities can identify relevant values and pertinent and contemporary issues where appropriate.

🇰🇪 Kenyan Context

Examples and activities can use appropriate Kenyan contexts while remaining relevant to the selected learning area.
